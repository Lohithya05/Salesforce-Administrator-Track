## 📚 Learnings from Day 2

During Day 2 of the Salesforce Administrator Track, I learned how Salesforce Reports and Dashboards help organizations analyze data and make informed business decisions.

### 🔹 Reports
- Learned how Salesforce Reports organize and display business data.
- Understood different report types and report filters.
- Explored how reports help track and analyze organizational performance.

### 🔹 Dashboards
- Learned how Dashboards provide a visual representation of report data.
- Explored dashboard components such as charts, tables, and metrics.
- Understood how dashboards help management monitor key business activities.

### 🔹 Business Analytics
- Learned how data is transformed into meaningful insights.
- Understood the importance of analytics in identifying trends and opportunities.
- Explored how organizations use data to improve performance.

### 🔹 KPI Tracking
- Learned about Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Understood how KPIs help measure organizational success.
- Explored how dashboards are used to track important business metrics.

### 🔹 Management Reporting
- Learned how managers use reports and dashboards for decision-making.
- Understood the role of reporting in monitoring institutional performance.
- Explored real-world reporting scenarios in Salesforce.

### 🎯 Key Takeaway
Salesforce Reports provide detailed information, while Dashboards transform that information into visual insights. Together, they help organizations monitor performance, track KPIs, and make data-driven decisions effectively.
