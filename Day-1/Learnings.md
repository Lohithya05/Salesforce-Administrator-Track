## 📚 Learnings from Day 1

During Day 1 of the Salesforce Administrator Track, I gained a strong understanding of Salesforce user management and security fundamentals.

### 🔹 Salesforce Administrator
- Learned the roles and responsibilities of a Salesforce Administrator.
- Understood how administrators manage users, permissions, and system security.

### 🔹 User Management
- Learned how to create, manage, activate, and deactivate users.
- Understood the importance of assigning the correct access to users.

### 🔹 Profiles, Roles, and Permission Sets
- Learned that **Profiles** define what a user can do.
- Learned that **Roles** define what data a user can see.
- Learned that **Permission Sets** provide additional permissions without changing profiles.
- Understood the differences between Profiles, Roles, and Permission Sets.

### 🔹 Identity and Login Management
- Learned the concepts of Authentication and Authorization.
- Explored Single Sign-On (SSO) and Identity Management.
- Understood how Salesforce verifies and manages user access.

### 🔹 Enterprise Security
- Learned the importance of the Principle of Least Privilege.
- Understood how organizations protect sensitive data using access controls.
- Explored the risks of providing unnecessary Administrator access.

### 🎯 Key Takeaway
Salesforce security is built around Users, Profiles, Roles, Permission Sets, and Identity Management. Proper access control ensures that the right users have access to the right data while maintaining enterprise security.
